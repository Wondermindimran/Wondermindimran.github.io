Five end-to-end projects covering real energy datasets; from raw data to cleaned CSVs, SQL analysis, Python visualisations, and interactive Tableau dashboards.

Solar Farm Performance Analysis

Analysed 34 days of 15-minute generation and irradiance data from a 30.46 MW utility-scale solar plant. Identified two inverters running 8–9% below the plant average, confirmed irradiance as the primary driver of output variation (Pearson R = 0.99), and quantified 151.7 MWh of recoverable generation loss across the 5 worst-performing days.

GitHub Dashboard

Battery Energy Storage System Operations

Extracted and analysed lifecycle data from NASA's battery aging experiment; converting complex MATLAB .mat files into structured CSVs. Tracked capacity fade, State of Health, and internal resistance growth across 168 discharge cycles. The battery crossed end-of-life at cycle 125 and ran 44 cycles beyond safe threshold; a risk quantified and turned into a monitoring recommendation.

Python scipy SQL Tableau NASA Dataset
GitHub Dashboard

Solar Hybrid Energy Mix Analysis

Analysed a full year of hourly HOMER Pro simulation data from a residential hybrid microgrid. Found that the system wastes 263.5 MWh of solar energy annually as excess, while running a generator 555 hours a year to fill the same evening gap that better battery storage could cover. The real problem was not insufficient solar. It was undersized storage.
